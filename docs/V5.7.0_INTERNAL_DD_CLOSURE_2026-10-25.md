## Release decision

1. **Close internal v5.7.0 due diligence with reservations**; archive evidence and retain the immutable tag.
2. **Permit only bounded single-host claims**, subject to verified production manifest, persistent registry, SQLite WAL budget, authorized configuration, integration qualification and documented residual risk acceptance. This report alone does not authorize deployment.
3. **Do not claim** production multi-host Raft, C3, external exactly-once effects, HITL safety guarantee, independent certification or Harv approval.
4. **Transfer the open items to 5.7.5** as a scoped follow-up; reserve FM-20 transport redesign for 5.8.0 unless an intervening critical finding changes release policy.
5. Any Harv finding reopens only its affected assurance claim and release gate; do not rewrite the published audit history.
